# Privacy Policy

**Project / Application Name**: `ebay-research-edge-phase2`  
**Effective Date**: June 1, 2026

## 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy describes how `ebay-research-edge-phase2` (the "Application") collects, uses, and handles data. The Application may access eBay-related account, listing, inventory, order, fulfillment, analytics, policy, learning, and change-management related data only as needed to provide its core functions and services.

## 2. Information Collected
In order to function properly, the Application may collect and process the following types of information:
- Account identifiers
- Listing, inventory, and offer data
- Order and fulfillment data
- Operational logs and audit records
- Configuration, policy, and change-management records

*Note: API credentials and authentication tokens are handled as confidential secrets and are never intentionally exposed publicly.*

## 3. How Information is Used
The information collected is used exclusively to:
- Operate and support marketplace workflows
- Evaluate listings and market data
- Manage incidents, operational policies, learning records, and controlled changes
- Maintain auditability, security, and overall service reliability

## 4. Sharing of Information
We value your privacy and restrict data sharing strictly to operational necessities:
- We **do not sell** any personal data.
- Information is only shared with service providers or platforms strictly required to operate the service (e.g., eBay APIs).
- We may disclose information when required by law or to protect our legal rights and safety.

## 5. Data Retention
We retain collected data only for as long as is reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, which includes ongoing operations, legal compliance, security monitoring, dispute handling, and audit purposes.

## 6. Security
We implement reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect the information we collect against unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. However, please be aware that no system can guarantee absolute security.

## 7. User Choices and Revocation
Users have control over their connected accounts. If you wish to disconnect the Application, you may revoke third-party app access at any time directly through your eBay account settings where applicable.

## 8. International Processing
Depending on the infrastructure utilized, the data we collect may be transferred to and processed in systems and services located across various international jurisdictions used to operate the Application.

## 9. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or operational requirements. Any updates will be posted directly to this repository.

## 10. Contact
For privacy-related questions, please contact the application operator through the repository contact channel or designated project contact.
